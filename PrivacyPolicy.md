## Summary

Movie DB is designed to keep your movie and TV library data under your control. Most data you enter or create in the app is stored locally on your device and, if you use iCloud, synced through Apple iCloud/CloudKit.

The app does not require a Movie DB account. The app does not send your movie titles, TV titles, notes, tags, ratings, watch state, or library identifiers to the developer's own server.

If you explicitly enable analytics, Movie DB sends pseudonymous usage analytics to PostHog using an app-generated analytics installation ID. This ID does not directly identify you by name or email, but it can distinguish one app installation from another and is therefore treated as personal data.

## Data You Store in the App

Movie DB may store information you add or generate while using the app, such as:

- Your saved movies and TV shows.
- Watch state and watch history.
- Ratings, notes, and tags.
- Custom lists, filters, and app settings.
- Imported data and related metadata.

This data is stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is enabled for the app, this data is also synced through Apple iCloud/CloudKit using your Apple account.

The developer does not operate a separate Movie DB user account system and does not receive this library data unless you choose to send it manually, for example in a support email.

## iCloud Sync

If iCloud sync is enabled, Apple processes the synced app data through iCloud/CloudKit so your data can be available across your devices.

Apple acts as an independent provider for iCloud services. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's own privacy terms and settings.

You can control iCloud availability and app access through iOS settings. If you disable iCloud for Movie DB, syncing may stop, but local data may remain on your device unless you delete it in the app or remove the app.

## The Movie Database (TMDB)

Movie DB uses The Movie Database (TMDB) to search for and display movie and TV metadata, images, and related content.

When you search, browse, or load details, the app may send requests to TMDB, including:

- Search terms you enter.
- Selected language or region settings.
- The requested movie, TV show, person, image, or video metadata endpoint.
- Standard technical request data such as your IP address, device/network metadata, and request time, as visible to TMDB when your device connects to TMDB.

TMDB may process this data according to its own privacy policy. Movie DB does not send your local notes, tags, personal ratings, or full local library contents to TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Images and Video Thumbnails

The app may load poster images, backdrop images, profile images, and trailer thumbnails from third-party content servers, including TMDB image servers and YouTube image servers.

When your device loads these resources, the relevant server may receive standard technical request data such as your IP address, the requested URL, device/network metadata, and request time.

## Purchases

Movie DB may offer purchases through Apple's App Store and StoreKit.

Purchase processing is handled by Apple. Apple may process your Apple ID, payment method, transaction data, and related account information according to Apple's own privacy policy.

Movie DB may store limited purchase status locally to unlock purchased features. The developer does not receive your full payment details.

## Optional Analytics

Analytics are disabled unless you explicitly enable them.

If you enable analytics, Movie DB uses PostHog to understand app usage, improve app quality, and diagnose product issues. PostHog is configured to use EU hosting.

Analytics data is linked to an app-generated analytics installation ID. The app displays this ID in the settings so you can include it when requesting access to or deletion of analytics data.

Analytics may include:

- App version, build, platform, OS family or OS major version, and broad device class.
- Screen or feature usage events.
- Feature flag exposure events.
- Anonymous purchase-related product identifiers or price metadata needed for analytics.
- Counts, buckets, durations, boolean values, and small predefined enum values.
- Import counts, error counts, and performance or reliability information.

Analytics do **not** include:

- Your name or email address.
- Movie or TV show titles.
- TMDB IDs from your library.
- Notes, tag names, or other free-form user content.
- Search text.
- File names or file paths.
- Personal ratings tied to specific media.
- URLs containing content data.

PostHog is configured to discard IP addresses. Analytics data is retained for 1 year.

You can disable analytics in the app settings. Disabling analytics stops future analytics events, but it does not automatically delete analytics data already stored by PostHog.

## Crash Reports and Diagnostics

Movie DB may receive crash reports and diagnostics through Apple and, if analytics is enabled, PostHog.

Crash and diagnostic data may include technical information such as app version, OS version, device model category, crash time, stack trace, and other technical details needed to diagnose app problems.

Crash reports are used to improve stability and fix bugs. They are not used for advertising or tracking.

## Support Emails

If you contact the developer by email, the developer processes the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message content, attachments, screenshots, device/app details, and any analytics installation ID you include.

Support emails are used to answer your request, diagnose issues, and maintain the app.

Do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

## Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, delete the app, or remove related local/iCloud data through Apple settings.

iCloud data retention is controlled through Apple iCloud and your Apple account settings.

PostHog analytics data is retained for 1 year.

Support emails are retained as long as needed to handle the request and for reasonable follow-up, recordkeeping, and legal purposes.

App Store purchase records are retained by Apple according to Apple's policies and legal obligations. Movie DB may retain local purchase entitlement status as needed to keep purchased features unlocked.
